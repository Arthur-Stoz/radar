Description

Kōwhai Café is a neighbourhood café on Ponsonby Road, open seven days from 6:30am. We roast in small batches and pour around 600 coffees on a Saturday.

What you’ll do
  • Pull consistent espresso on a two-group machine
  • Dial in the grinders each morning with the head barista
  • Keep the bar clean and help close on weekdays
What we offer
  • $27–29 an hour depending on experience, plus a share of tips
  • Free coffee on shift and a staff meal after five hours
  • Rosters out two weeks ahead

Open to working holiday visa holders with at least three months left on their visa.
